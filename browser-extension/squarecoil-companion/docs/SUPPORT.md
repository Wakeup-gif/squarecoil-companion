# SquareCoil Companion Support

SquareCoil Companion works on the US Sign & Mill SquareCoil website. For help, open the Companion panel, go to **Settings → Help and diagnostics**, and choose **Submit a ticket** or **Send feedback**. You can also email `cristian@ussignandmill.com`.

Include the Companion version, what you tried, and what happened. The toolbar popup's **Technical details** section can copy diagnostic status if useful; review it before sharing. Support and feedback open an email draft, which is sent only when you send it from your mail app. Please do not post customer names, job details, account credentials, backups, or timer exports in a public GitHub issue.

For privacy details, read the [SquareCoil Companion Privacy Policy](PRIVACY-POLICY.md).
