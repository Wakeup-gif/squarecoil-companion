# SquareCoil Companion Support

SquareCoil Companion works on the US Sign & Mill SquareCoil website. For help, use **Support / Feedback** in the extension or email `cristian@ussignandmill.com`.

Include the Companion version, what you tried, and what happened. The toolbar popup can copy technical status details if useful. Please do not post customer names, job details, account credentials, backups, or timer exports in a public GitHub issue.

For privacy details, read the [SquareCoil Companion Privacy Policy](PRIVACY-POLICY.md).
